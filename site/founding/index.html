What we ask in return

  1. Open it on a few real streams

    Keep StreamWing beside your room while you're live. You do all the chatting.

  2. Tell us what you noticed

    What helped, and what got in the way. You never need to share private messages.

  3. Stop any time

    If it isn't useful, say so and walk away. That tells us something too.

We're not promising more viewers or bigger tips. We're finding out if StreamWing is useful enough that you'd open it again.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Everything is free during early access and we don't ask for payment details.

What can StreamWing see?

It reads your room while you're live: who walks in, what's said in chat and who tips. It only reads.

Will it post in my room?

No. StreamWing can't post, tip or message as you. It can suggest a message, and you decide whether to send it yourself.

What if I change my mind?

Delete the link on Chaturbate and StreamWing loses access. You can also ask us to stop contacting you.
