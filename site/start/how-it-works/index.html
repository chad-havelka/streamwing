What it does

Six things StreamWing does, in plain words.

Each one works from the same room events. Nothing here posts, sends or changes anything in your room.

Remembers your regulars

StreamWing keeps a short record of people who visit your room: how often they come back and what they’ve tipped.

When they arrive again, you see their card and a one-line note from last time.

Calls out who’s back

When a regular, a big tipper, or someone with tokens but no tip yet arrives, your panel calls them out.

Use it to say hello at the right moment. You choose what to say.

Suggests a message

An optional small window beside your stream can suggest a short message when someone important arrives.

You copy it and send it yourself. StreamWing never types or sends for you.

Tracks each stream

Your panel shows tokens, tips and visitors for the stream you’re on, with a running tip log.

Growth over time is on the way.

Writes a recap

When a stream ends, you get a short recap: how long you streamed, who came through, and who to remember.

Notes are written after the stream, not during it.

Stays read-only

StreamWing only reads public room events. It keeps the last five chat messages from each viewer so it can remember them, and it never stores private messages.

Disconnecting removes your token, so StreamWing loses access straight away.

While you stream

Your panel shows who is here, and why it matters.

Open StreamWing on your phone or a second screen. It shows callouts as people arrive, so you can say hello to the right person at the right moment. You type every message yourself.

  • Regulars who came back
  • Big tippers who are back in the room
  • Viewers with tokens who haven’t tipped yet
  • A running tip log and session totals

After the stream

Next stream, StreamWing already knows them.

When a stream ends, StreamWing writes a short recap and a one-line note for the people who mattered. Those notes are there the next time they arrive, so you pick up where you left off.

Notes are written after each stream ends, not during it.

Your control

Read-only, and you decide what happens next.

  • StreamWing only reads public room events. It never posts, sends messages or touches your account.
  • We keep the last five chat messages from each viewer, so StreamWing can remember them. We never store private messages.
  • StreamWing never sends a message for you. It can suggest one, and you copy it and send it yourself.
  • Disconnect any time. Deleting the token on Chaturbate cuts StreamWing off straight away.
