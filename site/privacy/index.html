Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy covers the StreamWing website, streamwing.studio, and the StreamWing app performers open from their private link. The first part is about the signup and survey. The part headed "Inside the StreamWing app" is about performers who connect their Chaturbate room.

What we collect

  • What you type into the signup form: your streamer name, the platforms you stream on, and how you want us to reach you (an email address, a Telegram username or a WhatsApp number).
  • If you pick WhatsApp, whether you said yes to us messaging you there.
  • Your survey answers. If you leave the survey partway through, we keep the answers you gave up to that point.
  • The invite code in your link, if someone sent you one, so we know who referred you.

We don't ask for your legal name, your address or any payment details.

How we use it

  • To contact you about StreamWing on the channel you chose, including your early-access invite.
  • To understand what creators need and decide what to build.
  • To credit the person who referred you.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Signups and survey answers are stored with Netlify, the company that hosts this site. Like most websites, Netlify also keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

WhatsApp, Telegram and email

We only message you on WhatsApp if you ticked the box saying we can. You can tell us to stop at any time on any channel, and we will.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Reply to any message we've sent you and we'll take care of it.

If you are a viewer in a performer's room, write to us and we'll look up and delete what we hold about your Chaturbate username. There is no form for this yet, so it is handled by hand.

Inside the StreamWing app

When a performer connects her Chaturbate room, she gives StreamWing a link from Chaturbate that lets it read her room's events. It can only read. It never posts in her room and never touches her account.

  • Her Chaturbate link is stored encrypted. She can disconnect at any time, and deleting the token on Chaturbate cuts StreamWing off within about a minute.
  • What we keep about the viewers in her room: their Chaturbate username, when they came and went, what they tipped her, whether they followed her or joined her fan club, what Chaturbate reports about whether they have tokens, and the last five things they said in her public chat.
  • Private messages: we keep only the fact that one was sent, never what it said.
  • Her own notes about fans are hers. Only she sees them in the app.
  • AI notes are off until she turns them on. If she does, Claude, an AI service from Anthropic, writes a one-line note about each fan after a stream. She sees exactly what is sent before she switches it on. What is sent: the fan's username, what they tipped her, how many days they've visited, the last few things they said in her public chat, and her own note about them, if she wrote one. Private messages are never sent. Anthropic handles that data under its API terms.
  • Where it lives: on servers run by Railway. StreamWing's operators can see this data to run and fix the service. We don't sell it and don't share it with advertisers.
  • Deleting it: she can disconnect and delete everything StreamWing knows about her room, in one step, at any time. Her account name stays until she asks us to remove it.
  • Keeping it: we keep fan records until she deletes them. Nothing deletes them on a timer.

Who this is for

StreamWing is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change affects how we use information you already gave us, we'll tell you first.
