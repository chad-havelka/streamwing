What StreamWing handles while you're live

Your room's data, your call

Seven things we do with your room's data, and what each one means for you.

We only read your room

StreamWing reads your room's events through a link you create. It can't post, tip or message anyone as you.

What it means for you: Nobody speaks in your room under your name. Delete the link on Chaturbate and StreamWing loses access to your room.

We keep what your panel shows

Each regular's username, when they visited, what they tipped, whether they follow you or are in your fan club, and their last five public chat messages.

What it means for you: Your history with a regular is there when you need it. We never store what a private message says.

Your room link is encrypted

The Chaturbate link you paste is stored encrypted, not as plain text.

What it means for you: A copy of our database alone doesn't reveal your room link.

AI notes are off until you turn them on

If you turn them on, Claude, an AI service from Anthropic, writes a one-line note about each fan. You see exactly what is sent before you switch it on.

What it means for you: Nothing about your fans goes to an AI service unless you choose it.

We don't sell your fans' data

We don't sell it, and we don't share it with advertisers. Our team opens your data only to fix a problem the service has.

What it means for you: Your fan list stays your business record, not a product we sell.

Delete everything in one step

Disconnect and delete removes everything StreamWing holds about your room. It can't be undone.

What it means for you: You can leave at any time and nothing about your room stays in our database. Your account name stays until you ask us to remove it.

No automatic deletion

We keep fan records until you delete them. Nothing deletes them on a timer.

What it means for you: A regular's history is what makes the notes and birthdays work. A timer would throw it away.

Full detail is in our privacy policy.

Questions streamers ask

Does StreamWing post or message in my room?

No. It reads public room events from Chaturbate. It never posts, messages or sends anything in your name.

What is sent to Anthropic, and is it on by default?

AI notes are off until you turn them on. When you do, you see exactly what is sent first. Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, writes each note from a fan's username, days in your room, tips, fan club and follow status, whether they have tokens, their last five public chat messages, and your own note. Private messages are never sent.

How long do you keep fan records?

Until you delete them. Nothing deletes them on a timer. You can disconnect and delete everything StreamWing knows about your room in one step.

What happens when I disconnect?

Deleting the access token on Chaturbate cuts StreamWing off.

What do I need to connect my room?

The private link you were sent and a Chaturbate account. You make one access token, tick only the public box, and paste the link in. Nothing to install.

Can a viewer ask for their data to be removed?

Yes. They can write to us and we delete their record by hand. There is no form yet.

What does it cost?

Pricing isn't final yet. StreamWing is in early access and invite-only while we build.
