Questions

Do I need a card?

No. Everything is free during early access and we don't ask for payment details.

Do you take a cut of my tips?

No. Not on any plan. Solo Pro is a flat monthly price and Studio is a flat hourly rate.

What happens after early access?

Solo stays free. Solo Pro starts at the price on this page for rooms past the fan limit. We'll tell you before anything changes.

What will Studio cost?

The rate isn't set. Studio tools aren't built yet. We'll publish the rate and the cap before anyone is billed.

Does StreamWing do anything in my room?

No. It reads your room's events and shows them to you. It never posts, tips or changes anything.

How do I stop?

Delete the token on your Chaturbate token page. StreamWing loses access.
