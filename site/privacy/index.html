Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy covers the StreamWing website, streamwing.studio, as it works today: the early-access signup and the short survey after it. The StreamWing app isn't open yet. We'll update this policy before it is, and tell everyone on the list what changed.

What we collect

  • What you type into the signup form: your streamer name, the platforms you stream on, and how you want us to reach you (an email address, a Telegram username or a WhatsApp number).
  • If you pick WhatsApp, whether you said yes to us messaging you there.
  • Your survey answers. If you leave the survey partway through, we keep the answers you gave up to that point.
  • The invite code in your link, if someone sent you one, so we know who referred you.

We don't ask for your legal name, your address or any payment details.

How we use it

  • To contact you about StreamWing on the channel you chose, including your early-access invite.
  • To understand what creators need and decide what to build.
  • To credit the person who referred you.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Signups and survey answers are stored with Netlify, the company that hosts this site. Like most websites, Netlify also keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

WhatsApp, Telegram and email

We only message you on WhatsApp if you ticked the box saying we can. You can tell us to stop at any time on any channel, and we will.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Reply to any message we've sent you and we'll take care of it.

Who this is for

StreamWing is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change affects how we use information you already gave us, we'll tell you first.
