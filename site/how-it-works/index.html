Your room, your fans, your rules

StreamWing works quietly alongside you.

It never takes over your room or speaks on your behalf.

  • It reads public room events from Chaturbate.
  • It keeps the last five chat messages from each viewer, so it can remember them.
  • It never stores private messages.
  • It never posts or messages for you. You decide what to say and when. The only outside services it uses are Anthropic, for AI notes if you switch them on, and our uptime check, which sees your username when something breaks.
  • Disconnect whenever you want. Disconnecting removes your access token, so StreamWing stops reading straight away.

Want your data removed? Message us and we’ll handle it.

Questions streamers ask

Does StreamWing post or message in my room?

No. It reads public room events from Chaturbate. It never posts, messages or sends anything in your name.

What is sent to Anthropic, and is it on by default?

AI notes are off until you turn them on. When you do, you see exactly what is sent first. Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, writes each note from a fan's username, days in your room, tips, fan club and follow status, whether they have tokens, their last five public chat messages, and your own note. Private messages are never sent.

How long do you keep fan records?

Until you delete them. Nothing deletes them on a timer. You can disconnect and delete everything StreamWing knows about your room in one step.

What happens when I disconnect?

Deleting the access token on Chaturbate cuts StreamWing off.

What do I need to connect my room?

The private link you were sent and a Chaturbate account. You make one access token, tick only the public box, and paste the link in. Nothing to install.

Can a viewer ask for their data to be removed?

Yes. They can write to us and we delete their record by hand. There is no form yet.
