Your room's data, your call

Seven things we do with your room's data, and what each one means for you. The full policy is below.

We only read your room

StreamWing reads your room's events through a link you create. It can't post, tip or message anyone as you.

What it means for you: Nobody speaks in your room under your name. Delete the link on Chaturbate and StreamWing loses access to your room.

We keep what StreamWing shows you

Each regular's username, when they visited, what they tipped, whether they follow you or are in your fan club, and their last five public chat messages.

What it means for you: Your history with a regular is there when you need it. We never store what a private message says.

Your room link is encrypted

The Chaturbate link you paste is stored encrypted, not as plain text.

What it means for you: A copy of our database alone doesn't reveal your room link.

AI notes are off until you turn them on

If you turn them on, Claude, an AI service from Anthropic, writes a one-line note about each fan. You see exactly what is sent before you switch it on.

What it means for you: Nothing about your fans goes to an AI service unless you choose it.

We don't sell your fans' data

We don't sell it, and we don't share it with advertisers. Our team opens your data only to fix a problem the service has.

What it means for you: Your fan list stays your business record, not a product we sell.

Delete everything in one step

Disconnect and delete removes everything StreamWing holds about your room. It can't be undone.

What it means for you: You can leave at any time. Your fan records and history are gone. Your account record stays until you ask us to remove it.

No automatic deletion

We keep fan records until you delete them. Nothing deletes them on a timer.

What it means for you: A regular's history is what makes the notes and birthdays work. A timer would throw it away.

Privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the StreamWing website, streamwing.studio, and the StreamWing app performers open from their private link. The first part is about the signup and survey. The part headed "Inside the StreamWing app" is about performers who connect their Chaturbate room.

What we collect

  • What you type into the signup form: your streamer name, the platforms you stream on, and how you want us to reach you (an email address, a Telegram username or a WhatsApp number).
  • If you pick WhatsApp, whether you said yes to us messaging you there.
  • Your survey answers. If you leave the survey partway through, we keep the answers you gave up to that point.
  • The invite code in your link, if someone sent you one, so we know who referred you.

We don't ask for your legal name, your address or any payment details.

How we use it

  • To contact you about StreamWing on the channel you chose, including answers to your questions.
  • To understand what creators need and decide what to build.
  • To credit the person who referred you.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Signups and survey answers are stored with Netlify, the company that hosts this site. Like most websites, Netlify also keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

WhatsApp, Telegram and email

We only message you on WhatsApp if you ticked the box saying we can. You can tell us to stop at any time on any channel, and we will.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Reply to any message we've sent you and we'll take care of it.

If you are a viewer in a performer's room, write to us and we'll look up and delete what we hold about your Chaturbate username. There is no form for this yet, so it is handled by hand.

Inside the StreamWing app

When a performer connects her Chaturbate room, she gives StreamWing a link from Chaturbate that lets it read her room's events. It can only read. It never posts in her room and never touches her account.

  • Her Chaturbate link is stored encrypted. She can disconnect at any time, and deleting the token on Chaturbate cuts StreamWing off. Disconnecting stops StreamWing reading her room. Her fan records stay until she deletes them.
  • What we keep about the viewers in her room: their Chaturbate username, when they came and went, what they tipped her, whether they followed her or joined her fan club, what Chaturbate reports about whether they have tokens, and the last five things they said in her public chat.
  • Tip messages: stored in full, including any message attached to a tip.
  • Private messages: We record that a private message was sent. We never store what it says.
  • Her own notes about fans are hers. Only she sees them in the app.
  • AI notes are off until she turns them on. If she does, Claude, an AI service from Anthropic, writes a one-line note about each fan after a stream. She sees exactly what is sent before she switches it on. What is sent: the fan's username, days in her room, tokens tipped, biggest tip, whether they're in her fan club, whether they follow her, whether they have tokens, their last five public chat messages, and her own note about them. Private messages are never sent. Anthropic handles that data under its API terms.
  • Where it lives: on servers run by Railway. StreamWing's operators can see this data to run and fix the service. We don't sell it and don't share it with advertisers.
  • Deleting it: she can disconnect and delete everything StreamWing knows about her room, in one step, at any time. Her account record stays until she asks us to remove it. It holds her name, Chaturbate username, timezone and a hash of her access key, so her existing link still opens the panel, which shows she is disconnected. To fully cut access, also delete the token on her Chaturbate token page.
  • Keeping it: we keep fan records until she deletes them. Nothing deletes them on a timer.
  • Lost your link? Get a new one at /connect/lost/. The old link stops working.

Who this is for

StreamWing is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change affects how we use information you already gave us, we'll tell you first.
